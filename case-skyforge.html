SkyForge: Stratospheric Ozone Systems

Validating ozone generation at -60°C and FL400 altitudes.

Role: Systems Engineer

Tools: Arduino, C++, SolidWorks Flow Simulation, Ozone Sensors

The Challenge

Developing an ozone restoration payload for aircraft required the system to operate at 10–13 km altitude (Crusing Altitude), where temperatures drop to -60°C and air density is minimal.

The Approach

  • Simulation: Before cutting metal, I used SolidWorks Flow Simulation to iterate the housing geometry. The goal was to reduce the velocity of the incoming Mach 0.8 airflow to a laminar flow suitable for stable corona discharge.
  • Prototyping: Built a sensor integration testbed using Arduino. Integrated high-sensitivity sensors for Ozone (O3), Flow Rate, Pressure, and Temperature.
  • Power: Modified a standard HV power supply to maintain stable arc discharge under varying load conditions.

Result

Validated the aerodynamic design and sensor fusion logic. Established the baseline architecture for the "In-Flight Ozone Restoration" concept (Provisional Patent work).
