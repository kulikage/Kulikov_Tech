Offshore and hazardous-environment machinery

Heavy machinery, hydraulic actuation and remotely operated systems for work at sea and in high-radiation zones, from my prior engineering experience as a lead design engineer.

Role
Lead design engineer
Period
2014–2021
Tools
SolidWorks, KOMPAS-3D, kinematic analysis

Motion-compensated offshore gangway

Context. A gangway that transfers personnel between vessels and offshore oil platforms in rough seas, similar in purpose to systems such as Ampelmann.

Contribution. Led the 3D modeling of the hydraulic hexapod platform and developed the control-logic flowchart for active wave compensation.

Outcome. Completed the initial prototype design and its kinematic verification.

Remote-controlled robot for high-radiation zones

Context. A robotic platform for operations where radiation would damage unprotected electronics.

Contribution. Designed the internal component layout and calculated the lead and composite shielding thicknesses needed to protect sensitive electronics without exceeding the weight limit.

Outcome. Delivered subsystem layouts for the master assembly.
