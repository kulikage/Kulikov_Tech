Selected case studies

Each one follows the same thread: the problem, how I approached it, and what came out of it.

Production TPU seals on a modified FDM printer

Own product: intercooler seals for diesel engines

Problem
The seals needed soft TPU (Shore 65A), and standard FDM extruders jam on filament that soft at production volume.
Approach
Rebuilt a printer around a direct-drive flexible-filament path, a 32-bit board with TMC2209 drivers and reworked Marlin firmware.
Impact
≈3,000 kits sold and 15,000+ components made, from design through production and sales.

TorrideX energy conversion

Kulicage Dynamics R&D program

Problem
Compact, long-duration power that turns heat directly into electricity, with no moving parts.
Approach
Plasma-assisted thermionic conversion, developed simulation-first: models are checked against analytical references before they size hardware.
Status
Modeling and numerical verification. Next: a non-radioactive, electrically heated bench experiment.

Kulicage Dynamics LLC / TorrideX is a member of the NVIDIA Inception program.

How I work

An R&D loop that spends simulation time to save hardware iterations.

  1. 01

    Hypothesis

    Define the physical limit to beat and what a working result has to show.

  2. 02

    Simulation

    CFD, FEA and thermal models, checked against analytical references, to cut physical iterations.

    COMSOL, ANSYS, OpenFOAM, Python, NVIDIA Warp

  3. 03

    Prototyping

    Rapid fabrication: FDM and SLA printing, CNC, bench electronics.

  4. 04

    Validation

    Instrumented tests, data analysis in Python, failure analysis and design fixes.

Validation results feed the next hypothesis.
