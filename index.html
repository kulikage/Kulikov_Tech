TorrideX Energy

Plasma Physics • Simulation-Guided Energy Conversion

  • Problem: Long-duration autonomous power is limited by battery replacement, fuel logistics, solar dependence, and low conversion efficiency in harsh environments.
  • Approach: Developing TorrideX Small as a non-radioactive laboratory validation platform for staged direct and thermoelectric extraction.
  • Status: Simulation-guided development path with pending U.S. patent filings and laboratory MVP preparation.
